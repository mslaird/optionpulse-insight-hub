# OptionPulse.io: Predictive Options Analytics (MVP)
*Founder & builder · shipped TypeScript + Supabase MVP · 2025 (paused)*

**One-liner:** A predictive options-analytics MVP with AI signal modeling built, shipped, and validated with early users before I made the call to pause on data-cost and regulatory grounds.

---

## What it did
Surfaced predictive signals and analytics on options activity, with AI-driven modeling to flag notable setups for retail options traders.

## What I built
- A working **MVP** in **TypeScript + Supabase** (auth, data layer, UI) with an AI signal-modeling layer.
- Ran it in front of real users to test demand.

## The honest part (why it matters)
Secured **early interest**, then paused after validation when two structural walls became clear: **market-data API costs** at indie scale and **regulatory** exposure around financial signals. I chose to stop rather than fund a model whose economics didn't close.

**What it demonstrates:** I ship real software (not just decks), put it in front of users, read the signal honestly, and make disciplined kill/continue decisions the exact judgment startups want in an early PM or solutions engineer.
